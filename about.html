Our story

Capital, debt and taxes have stayed with people since time immemorial. Wars have been won on them, families broken, rebellions raised and societies shaped by how they were handled. It is far easier to make wealth than to keep it. Money looks like the dullest thing there is: it only changes hands. Follow it further, through the network that carries it round the world and into the minds it moves, and its right use turns into something else: wealth that compounds, and outlives a generation. That change is our work. Our parents’ generation was mostly not born with wealth. They built it as the economy opened its doors, and kept it with sanskaar, the values you are raised inside. Metamorph grew out of a chartered accountancy practice with thirty years of signed accounts, and was founded by Ritvik and Gurleen to stand beside founders who are driven, hungry and growing. We are here for founders and promoters; for investors, from angels to family offices and venture capital; for creatives and curious souls, data and knowledge geeks, and anyone curious about the world. Money is a double-edged sword: it builds, and it brings out greed, theft and treachery. Everyone wants to be rich. We believe in wealth and despise the relentless, unhinged chase for it. A firm becomes what the people who run it want; ours earn, spend, invest and save with clarity and a right conscience, and look for founders who do the same. Every empire that stops growing starts declining, so we work where there is growth, ambition and a right conscience, and only as a premium service: we would rather advise fewer companies well than many quickly.
