Fundraising and investor readiness.

Three principles, and the eight things we do to raise the capital.

PrinciplesEvery raise passes through three hands.

CreatorNegotiatorCloser
i

Deal creator.

Investors follow honest intent, promoter clarity and fiscal discipline. We weave them together for the organisation, and bring the capital together with expertise from the investor network.

ii

Deal negotiator.

There are thousands of investors and financiers, each with their own thesis and risk-return profile. Negotiating the best deal with the right investor brings the precision that quality business building needs.

iii

Deal closer.

An end-to-end deal closer: all the preparation, presenting the case to the investors in our network, and finalising the deal that is best for the company and the investor.
